Getting Kalpa Known: The Next 90 Days

For Prashant and Gautam, from Priyanka and Khaled

Hey Prashant,

Great chatting, and thanks for walking us through Kalpa. You beat ElevenLabs on realism and you’re about to plug into Vapi, but most voice AI builders haven’t heard of you yet. That’s the gap we want to close.

So here’s what Khaled and I would actually do. Social is the main engine: we’d run your and Gautam’s X and LinkedIn every week. We’d pull a couple of stunts and events that get people talking. And since you’re both technical founders, we’d help you build a real sales process so the intros and demos that come in actually turn into customers.

Warm intros stay your main source of meetings. We’d keep a small amount of outbound running alongside them to add extra demos.

Our philosophy

We really care about our craft. Since everything is still early, we can only guarantee inputs, not outputs. What we do guarantee is best in class inputs: social, events, sales coaching, outbound and assets.

The main thing

Social on X and LinkedIn

This is where Kalpa gets known. Khaled leads it, we write everything, and you approve before anything goes out.

One 30 minute call a week

We interview you or Gautam about what you shipped, learned or tested, and turn it into the week’s posts.

Posts every week

X for voice AI builders (demos, benchmarks, technical takes). LinkedIn for the founder story, partners and investors.

Distribution

We get posts in front of the right people: replying in voice AI threads, tagging partners, and boosting through our own network.

The first five posts we’d write

  • The blind test. Two clips, one Kalpa and one ElevenLabs. Guess which is which. Reveal in the replies.
  • Why one model for every audio task. Prashant on why Kalpa does cloning, editing, dubbing and understanding in one model instead of five.
  • The founder story. From Google and quant trading to YC, and why you two decided to build a voice model.
  • The weird demo. Clone a voice, add an accent, then make it sing, in one prompt. Short video, no explanation needed.
  • The Vapi launch. When the integration goes live: a demo, a post from both sides, and a reason for builders to try it that week.

Posts we’ve written for founders

Stunts and events

Social gets louder when there’s something real to post about. We plan and run these ourselves.

Khaled delivered croissants to SF brands

He showed up in person at the companies he wanted to work with, croissants in hand. It got people talking online and opened doors that cold messages wouldn’t. See the post.

That’s the kind of thing we’d do for Kalpa. Some ideas:

  • Blind taste test booth at voice AI and dev events. People guess Kalpa vs ElevenLabs, and we film it.
  • Voice AI builder dinner in SF for 10 to 15 people building voice agents, co-hosted with Vapi.
  • A voice drop to the founders you most want as customers: a personal message in their own cloned voice (with permission).

Sales coaching for two technical founders

You’ve built a better model. Selling it is a different skill, and it’s one we’ve done for years. We’d set up a sales process you can actually run.

Review your calls

We listen to your recorded sales calls and send back notes after each one: what landed, what to cut, what to ask next time.

Join live demos

We sit in on early demos with you, then debrief right after.

Build the sales process

Discovery questions, a demo flow that ends with a next step, how to talk pricing, and follow-ups that don’t go cold.

Tighten the pitch

A one or two sentence explanation of Kalpa that a developer gets immediately and repeats to their team.

For context: I was the first sales hire at Securitize through its IPO, and I’ve helped scale companies to $3M+ ARR. Your calls get the same attention we’d give our own.

Outbound and assets, always on

Warm intros lead. Alongside them, we keep a steady outbound campaign running to teams already building voice agents on Vapi, Pipecat and similar platforms. We build our own lists and write every message for that company. The goal is extra demos, not replacing the intros.

We also make the assets that every intro, post and demo needs:

Marketing

A benchmark page with side by side audio, homepage copy, demo clips.

Sales

A one-pager, a sales deck, and a short blurb that makes warm intros easy to forward.

Proof

A case study with your first integration partner or customer, once there’s one to tell.

  • Outbound starts
MONTH 2

MONTH 3

Turn It into Demos

How we’d work together

Two to three calls a week to go over what went out, what we learned and what’s next, plus a shared Slack channel.

The goal after 90 days: people in voice AI know the name Kalpa, you have a content engine that runs every week, a sales process you’re confident in, and more demos coming in from intros and outbound.

If this direction makes sense, let’s go through it on our next call and pick where to start.
