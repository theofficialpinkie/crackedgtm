Getting Kalpa Known: A Brand Plan for the Next 90 Days

For Prashant and Gautam, from Priyanka and Khaled

Hey Prashant,

Great chatting, and thanks for walking us through Kalpa. Beating ElevenLabs on realism benchmarks is a big deal, and the Vapi integration you’re working on puts you right where voice AI builders already are.

What stuck with me is that the product is ahead of the brand. Right now the people who should know about Kalpa mostly don’t. So I think the job for the next 90 days is pretty simple: make Kalpa the name voice AI builders bring up when they talk about the best sounding voices.

You mentioned warm intros are where most of your pipeline will come from, and I agree that should stay the main engine. The brand work makes those intros land faster, because people have already heard of you by the time the intro shows up. We’d still suggest a small amount of targeted outreach on the side, just to pick up extra demos and meetings.

Here’s how Khaled and I would approach it. It’s a starting point, and we’d adjust as we learn.

Our philosophy

We really care about our craft. Since everything is still early, we can only guarantee inputs, not outputs. What we do guarantee is best in class inputs: social, events, copy, website and outreach.

Telling the story

Before we turn up the volume, we’d tighten what people hear. A one line explanation of Kalpa that a developer can repeat to a friend, and proof they can check themselves.

One line pitch

A short, plain explanation of what Kalpa is and why it sounds better, used everywhere.

Benchmark page

Turn the Audio Realism results into a page people share, with side by side audio clips anyone can listen to.

Website

Rework the homepage opening so a developer gets it in five seconds and can hear the voices right away.

Demo clips

A small library of short, shareable audio and video clips we can use across posts, events and intros.

Founder content on X and LinkedIn

We’d do a short call with you each week and turn it into posts for you and Gautam. Khaled leads this side, and you approve everything before it goes out.

X is where voice AI builders hang out, so that’s where we’d lean in hardest: blind listening tests, benchmark drops, behind the scenes on how you train the model, and quick demos of things only Kalpa can do. LinkedIn would carry the founder story (ex-Google and quant backgrounds, YC, building a better voice model) for investors, partners and the people who make intros.

Events and stunts

The fastest way to build a name in voice is to let people hear it. We’d run a couple of moments that get people talking, and pick based on where the right builders are most likely to show up.

The blind taste test

A booth or table at voice AI and dev events where people guess which clip is Kalpa and which is ElevenLabs. Great in person, and even better as a video afterward.

Voice AI builder night

A small SF dinner or hack night for people building voice agents, co-hosted with a partner like Vapi so the right crowd shows up.

Integration launch

When the Vapi integration goes live, we’d treat it like a real launch: posts from both sides, a demo video, and a reason for builders to try it that week.

Something less conventional

Like Khaled delivering croissants to SF brands. For Kalpa it could be cloning a well known founder’s voice (with permission) for a demo people can’t stop sharing.

Warm intros first, with a little outreach on the side

Warm intros stay the main engine. We’d help you map who in your network (YC, investors, Vapi and other partners) can open doors to voice agent companies, and write a short forwardable blurb so every intro is easy to make.

On the side, we’d run a small, targeted outreach campaign to teams already building voice agents on Vapi, Pipecat and similar platforms. Every message written for that company, not blasted to thousands of people. The goal there is just extra demos and meetings, not replacing the intros.

The 90 days

MONTH 1

Tell the Story

  • One line pitch and homepage
  • Benchmark page and demo clips
  • Founder content starts on X and LinkedIn
  • Map warm intros
MONTH 2

Make Some Noise

  • Vapi integration launch
  • First event or stunt
  • Blind listening test campaign
  • Light outreach for extra demos
MONTH 3

Turn Attention into Demos

  • Scale what’s working
  • Second event or stunt
  • Follow up with everyone who engaged
  • Write down the playbook

How we’d work together

We’d do two to three calls a week to go over what went out, what we learned and what’s next, plus a shared Slack channel.

The goal after 90 days: people in voice AI know the name Kalpa, a content engine that keeps running, a couple of moments people remember, and more demos coming in from both intros and outreach.

If this direction makes sense, we can go through it together on our next call and figure out where to start.
