Leeroo × Cracked GTM

A 90-day plan to find Leeroo’s next enterprise customers

For Majid, Alireza and Arshad · from Priyanka and Khaled

Hey Majid,

Great chatting last week, and thanks for walking us through Leeroo. The telecom project is impressive, especially the scope of the work and how quickly you delivered it.

I think the next step is finding 2 to 3 more enterprise customers with similar problems. That’s what will tell us which buyers, use cases and messages are repeatable.

Here’s how Khaled and I would approach the next 90 days. It’s a starting point, and we’d adjust as we learn.

01 · Case study

Turn the telecom project into a real case study

This is a big deal with serious buyers, so the case study needs to feel as sophisticated as the work. We’d make it visual and easy to share.

1

Interview the customer

Learn what problem they needed solved, why they chose Leeroo, and which results mattered most to them.

2

Write and design it

Built around their problem and the outcome.

3

Homepage and pitch

Rewrite your homepage opening and put together a one-page pitch you can use in enterprise calls.

02 · Sales coaching

Onboarding and sales coaching

Leeroo does a lot, which is a good problem to have. But on a first call the buyer needs the short version. The goal is a pitch tight enough that someone can repeat it to their boss after one conversation.

We’d also spend real time on discovery calls: what to ask, how to qualify, and how to run a demo that ends with a next step. It makes a big difference on enterprise deals.

Day 1

Positioning

Who it’s for, what it does, the 1 to 2 sentence version.

Day 2

Discovery and demos

Role-playing real calls until it feels natural.

Day 3

Sales assets

One-pager, deck, follow-up emails, and a simple process to run sales.

03 · Outbound

Find your next 2 to 3 enterprise customers

We’d go after companies that look like your telecom customer and have a reason to move now, like an active legacy migration, open data platform roles, or a data leader posting about AI projects stalling. Banking, finance and healthcare are where we’d look first.

We build the lists and do the research ourselves (scraping, enrichment, a lot of automation), so every message is written for that company instead of blasted to thousands of people. For these first few, I’d lead with the outcome (getting the project done) more than the platform.

04 · Content and one creative campaign

Build a presence with the people who buy Leeroo

We’d do a short call with you each week and turn it into 2 to 3 LinkedIn posts for you and Alireza. Khaled leads this side, and you approve everything before it goes out.

The posts would be written for the people who actually buy Leeroo, probably CIOs, heads of data and innovation leaders at large companies in telecom, banking and healthcare. A few we’d start with:

  • What we learned moving 1,000+ tables and 100+ Power BI reports in 8 weeks
  • Why general-purpose agents plateau on specialized data work
  • What a CIO should ask before letting AI agents near production data
  • Why your agents should learn inside your own cloud, and why that matters for banks and hospitals

One creative campaign

We’d also run one creative campaign to get in front of buyers who are hard to reach through outbound. That could be a small dinner with 8 to 12 data leaders in London, or something less conventional, like delivering croissants to target accounts. We’ve done both before, and we’d pick based on where the right buyers are most likely to engage.

The 90 days
MONTH 1

Build GTM Systems

  • Customer interview
  • Case study and homepage
  • Sales coaching
  • First outbound and content
MONTH 2

Schedule Meetings Consistently

  • Test new segments
  • Discovery and demos together
  • Creative campaign
  • Follow-up automations
MONTH 3

Improve Sales Process

  • Scale what’s working
  • Support live deals
  • Refine who you sell to
  • Write down the playbook

Our rough target is 10 to 15 qualified demos, but we’ll revisit that after Month 1 once we have real numbers.

How we’d work together

One call a week, one shared channel

We’d do one call a week to go over what went out, what we learned and what’s next, plus a shared Slack channel.

The goal after 90 days

A clearer pitch, proof from more customers, a repeatable way to generate qualified conversations, and a better understanding of what it takes to close the next enterprise deal.

If this direction makes sense, we can go through it together on our call and figure out where to start.

Priyanka & Khaled
