Launching GTM: Leeroo’s GTM Plan for the Next 90 Days

For Majid, Alireza and Arshad, from Priyanka and Khaled

Hey Majid,

It was great chatting last week and thanks for walking us through Leeroo. Honestly the telecom project is super impressive, especially how much you guys got done and how fast.

I think the big thing right now is landing 2 to 3 more enterprise customers with similar problems. That’s how we figure out who’s actually buying, which use cases land and what messaging works, so the telecom deal isn’t a one-off.

Here’s roughly how Khaled and I would think about the next 90 days. Nothing here is set in stone, we’ll learn a ton as we go and adjust.

Our philosophy

We really care about our craft. That said, it’s still early, so we can’t guarantee outputs like a set number of meetings or deals yet. What we can guarantee is best in class inputs: outbound, social, copy, website and sales assets. We’ll do those really well every week and be super transparent about what’s working and what’s not.

01 · Case study

Turn the telecom project into a real case study

This was a big deal with serious buyers, so the case study should feel just as polished as the work you did. We’d make it really visual and easy to share around.

1

Talk to your customer

Figure out what problem they were trying to solve, why they went with you guys, and what results they actually cared about.

2

Write and design it

Tell the story around their problem and what changed after.

3

Homepage and pitch

Rewrite the top of your homepage and put together a one-pager you can use on enterprise calls.

02 · Sales coaching

Onboarding and sales coaching

Leeroo does a lot, which is honestly a good problem to have. But on a first call buyers just need the short version. We want the pitch tight enough that someone could repeat it to their boss after one call.

We’d also spend real time on discovery calls, like what to ask, how to qualify, and how to run a demo that actually ends in a next step. This makes a huge difference on enterprise deals.

Day 1

Positioning

Who it’s for, what it does, and how to say it in 1 to 2 sentences.

Day 2

Discovery and demos

Role-playing real calls until it feels natural.

Day 3

Sales assets

One-pager, deck, follow-up emails, and a simple process to run sales.

03 · Outbound

Find your next 2 to 3 enterprise customers

We’d go after companies that look a lot like your telecom customer and have a reason to buy now. Think an active legacy migration, open data platform roles, or a data leader posting about AI projects getting stuck. I’d start with banking, finance and healthcare.

We build the lists and do all the research ourselves (scraping, enrichment, lots of automation), so every message is written for that specific company, not blasted out to thousands of people. For the first few, I’d lead with getting the project done vs. pitching the platform.

04 · Content and one creative campaign

Founder content and one creative campaign

We’d hop on a quick call with you each week and turn it into 2 to 3 LinkedIn posts for you and Alireza. Khaled runs this side and you approve everything before it goes out.

The posts would be for the people actually buying Leeroo, probably CIOs, heads of data and innovation leads at big telecom, banking and healthcare companies. Some ideas to start:

  • What we learned moving 1,000+ tables and 100+ Power BI reports in 8 weeks
  • Why general-purpose agents hit a wall on specialized data work
  • What a CIO should ask before letting AI agents touch production data
  • Why your agents should learn inside your own cloud (and why banks and hospitals care)

One creative campaign

We’d also do one creative campaign to get in front of buyers who don’t really respond to outbound. Could be a small dinner with 8 to 12 data leaders, or something a little more out there like dropping off croissants at target accounts. We’ve done both, and we’d pick whichever fits where your buyers actually are.

The 90 days
MONTH 1

Build GTM Systems

  • Customer interview
  • Case study and homepage
  • Sales coaching
  • First outbound and content
MONTH 2

Schedule Meetings Consistently

  • Test new segments
  • Discovery calls and demos together
  • Creative campaign
  • Follow-up automations
MONTH 3

Improve Sales Process

  • Double down on what’s working
  • Help close live deals
  • Tighten who you sell to
  • Write down the playbook

We’d aim for 10 to 15 qualified demos, but we’ll revisit that after month 1 once we see real numbers.

How we’d work together

A weekly call and a shared Slack

We’d do a call every week to go over what went out, what we learned and what’s next, and keep a shared Slack channel for everything in between.

Where we want to be after 90 days

A pitch that’s way easier to explain, a few more customers to point to, a steady flow of good conversations, and a much better sense of what it takes to close the next big deal.

If this sounds right, let’s go through it on our call and figure out where to start.

Priyanka & Khaled
