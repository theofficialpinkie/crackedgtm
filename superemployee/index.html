Investor memo · Confidential

Superemployee is the infrastructure that enables top operators to earn $1M a year independently.

Problem

Startups spend heavily to recruit full-time talent, while experienced operators increasingly want to work independently.

Going solo is difficult. Operators need a brand, systems to serve multiple clients, and a consistent pipeline.

Solution

We give operators:

  • AI-powered systems to scale their work
  • A community that shares knowledge and referrals
  • Opportunities to earn startup equity

Startups get access to proven operators without making a full-time hire.

Why now

AI agents make it easier for one operator to deliver high-quality work across multiple companies.

Network effects

Operators can make $1M a year and hold small pieces of equity in every company that joins the platform. Startups get access to guaranteed top-tier talent and high-quality work. Each new company makes the platform more valuable for operators, and each new operator makes it more valuable for companies.

Business model

Superemployee charges a 25% platform fee and receives a small equity stake from each participating startup.

1,000 superemployees × $1M a year × 25% = $250M a year.
